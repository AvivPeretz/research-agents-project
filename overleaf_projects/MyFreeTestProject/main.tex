\section{Introduction}
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, decision-making, and language understanding. In recent years, AI has advanced rapidly due to increased computing power, large datasets, and improved algorithms.

A central area within AI is machine learning, where systems learn patterns from data instead of following fixed instructions. Another important approach is deep learning, which uses neural networks to perform complex tasks such as image recognition and speech processing.

Today, AI is used in many areas including healthcare, finance, transportation, and education. Despite its many benefits, AI also raises challenges related to privacy, bias, and ethical use. Therefore, developing responsible and transparent AI systems has become an important goal for researchers and organizations.
